# The Lifecycle Offsite: Three Days, One Island, Zero Open Tabs

*A team itinerary prepared with the rigor of a quarterly review and the sun protection of a first draft.*

## Day 1: Onboarding

**8:00 AM | Arrival and Welcome Series**
Landing triggers the first message in a five-part welcome series, delivered in person by a man holding a sign. Open rates are expected to be high, as he is standing right there.

**10:00 AM | Hotel Check-In and Segmentation**
Guests are segmented by room type, floor, and tolerance for ocean-view upsells. Expansion revenue begins at the minibar.

**12:00 PM | Lunch on the Lanai**
Seating has been arranged so the best view sits above the fold. Anyone placed below it may scroll.

**2:00 PM | Lei-Making Workshop**
Teams string plumeria and orchids into leis, a hands-on lesson in nurture. Like any onboarding drip, each lei is slow, fragrant, and prone to tangling.

**5:00 PM | Sunset Pipeline Review**
The forecast will be reviewed at sunset, ideal lighting for any number that needs to look warm. Pipeline is measured in coconuts, and coverage is currently described as generous.

## Day 2: Expansion

**7:30 AM | Wireframe Yoga**
Participants hold poses drawn in low fidelity. Downward dog will be rendered in grayscale until approved.

**9:30 AM | Snorkeling and Net Revenue Retention**
Everyone is counted entering the water and again on exit. Anyone drifting toward open water will be classified as churn and gently re-engaged by a lifeguard.

**12:00 PM | Botanical Garden Tour**
A guide introduces the hibiscus, the bird of paradise, and a plumeria tree that blooms without a single nurture email. Questions are welcome, but the tree will not be taking any.

**2:30 PM | Shave Ice A/B Test**
Group A receives lime and Group B receives mango. Statistical significance is reached when someone says "the orange one" and nobody corrects them.

**4:30 PM | Publish Day at the Beach**
Teams build a sandcastle, publish it at low tide, and verify its breakpoints against the incoming wave. Rollback is automatic.

**7:00 PM | Luau and Quarterly Business Review**
Dinner doubles as a quarterly review of the roast pig, which has been in the oven since the previous quarter. Slides are optional; napkins are not.

## Day 3: Win-Back

**8:30 AM | Farewell Brunch**
The buffet is organized like a sitemap: eggs to the left, pastries to the right, and a table of untouched melon that returns a 404.

**10:00 AM | Waterfall Hike**
The trail loads slowly but rewards patience. Load times are measured in switchbacks, and the view is considered a strong above-average experience.

**12:00 PM | Souvenir Shop Win-Back Campaign**
Anyone who abandoned a snack in their cart on Day 1 will receive a win-back offer: a second chance and a magnet.

**2:00 PM | Hibiscus Tea and Annual Review**
Annual recurring revenue will be discussed over hibiscus tea, in annual recurring pineapples. Please direct questions to the suggestion box, which is shaped like a lei.

**4:00 PM | Lei Exchange**
Each attendee gives one lei and receives one, a perfectly balanced trade and the only forecast this week with no variance.

## Packing List

- Reef-safe sunscreen (ownership addressed below)
- One floral shirt, hibiscus print preferred, dignified
- A swimsuit and a backup swimsuit
- Walking shoes for the waterfall hike
- A laptop you intend not to open
- A reusable water bottle
- A phone charger and a second phone charger
- A printed copy of the forecast, for shade

## Logistics

- **Dress code:** Business casual, with an emphasis on casual and a minimum of one flower.
- **Wi-Fi:** Available at the pool, the lobby, and the pond, where a small golden goose has been asked to stop sitting on the router.
- **Sunscreen:** Owned by whoever brought it, which makes it a shared service with no roadmap.
- **Out-of-office:** Please set auto-replies to "back Monday, fragrant."
